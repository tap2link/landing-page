## Moerschen use case general overview

<!-- eyebrow: About -->

In recent months we teamed up with Moerschen, a top player in the agribusiness world, to bring together a digital solution to their farmers—now a tap2link Users. Our versatile platform that works on both mobile (iOS and Android) and web, uses NFC technology to make managing agricultural machinery and services a light breeze. By automating processes, keeping a close eye on every service request, spare part, and penny spent we’re boosting productivity of both farmers and Moerschen employees. Think of it as the Swiss Army knife of farm management tools!

## Results

Discover how our partnership with Moerschen helped farmers manage their equipment and streamline service requests through tap2link solutions.

![A Moerschen profile from the tap2link app.](/images/use-cases/moerschen-profile.png)

### Enhanced productivity and automation

By embracing tap2link profiles feature, Moerschen has given their farmers the superpower of personalised digital spaces where they can store all the nitty-gritty details about their shiny machines.

These profiles come with QR code stickers that can be slapped onto the machines. Just scan the code, and voilà! You have access to product manuals, you can request services, and even track how those services are coming along—all in real-time. This automation means less time fiddling with paperwork and more time doing what farmers do best. It’s like having a personal assistant who never sleeps (and doesn’t require coffee breaks)!

![A Moerschen sticker with a QR code that can be attached to machines.](/images/use-cases/moerschen-sticker.png)

### Streamlined service requests and maintenance tracking

One of the coolest features of tap2link is how it makes service requests easy. Farmers can start a service request by simply scanning the QR code on their machinery. This action zips the request straight into the tap2link app, creating a direct line of communication with Moerschen's service team. The app not only keeps tabs on the status of each service request but also logs a detailed history of all maintenance activities. This transparency and organisation mean less downtime for machines and more uptime for farming.

![Screenshot der Service geschichten-Seite von unserem Partner Moerschen](/images/use-cases/moerschen-confirm-product.png)

### Comprehensive cost management

Tap2link's integration with Moerschen doesn't stop at making service requests a walk in the park; it also puts a lot of clarity at cost management. Farmers can track every spare part used in their machinery, logging each transaction in their tap2link profiles. This feature offers a full view of all expenses, helping farmers manage their budgets without breaking a sweat. Monitoring and analysing these costs helps farmers make smart decisions about future purchases and maintenance, ultimately leading to some serious savings. It's all about making every penny count, ensuring that every dollar spent is an investment in their farming success.
