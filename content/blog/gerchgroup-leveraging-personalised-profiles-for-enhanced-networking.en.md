## Fully personalised company profile

As one of Germany's leading project and district developers for large-volume construction projects, Gerchgroup can recognise development opportunities where others see obstacles. With this motto is mind, they decided to use tap2link potential in their networking efforts and get rid of paper business cards once and for all.

## Management made easier with one-tap solutions

After several meetings and discussions, t2l design team gained a clear understanding of Gerchgroup unique style and branding and proceeded to create personalised company profile, customised to their needs. That main profile was designed to capture the essence of the company's brand and create a lasting impression on potential customers.Alongside new and improved online presence, Gerchgroup representatives are now also equipped with custom designed, nfc-powered business cards linked to their individual employee profiles which are managed directly from the admin page. With 29 members integrated, custom made, plastic business cards were produced, just in time for their industrial fair.

## Individual profiles managed from admin panel

With a never-stopping and always evolving company such as the Gerchgroup, there’s a constant growth in the business development area but also swift changes in the number of employees and their role in the company. With the custom build like t2l company profile, every individual employee profile can managed from the admin page within just a few seconds, same goes for adding new team members.

## There’s more to come

By utilising personalised business profiles, Gerchgroup experienced a significant improvement in networking opportunities during industrial fairs. The visually appealing and informative combination of personalised business cards and company’s tap2link profile attracted the attention of visitors and initiated conversations, leading to valuable connections and potential business opportunities.In the next steps, we’re working on delivering a high quality, design driven metal cards which will convey a sense of sophistication and professionalism, aligning with the company's desired image.
